What is RAG?

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) is a technique that makes AI smarter by giving it access to your specific knowledge — your documents, data, and content — before it generates a response.

The problem RAG solves

Large Language Models (LLMs) like GPT and Gemini are trained on vast amounts of public data. They're incredibly capable, but they have limitations:

  • Knowledge cutoff — They don't know about events or data after their training date
  • No personal context — They can't access your company's internal documents, your research notes, or your private data
  • Hallucination — When they don't know something, they sometimes generate plausible-sounding but incorrect information
  • Generic responses — Without specific context, answers tend to be broad rather than precisely relevant

💡 The core idea

Instead of relying solely on what the AI was trained on, RAG retrieves relevant information from your own documents and includes it in the prompt. The AI then generates answers grounded in your actual data.

How RAG works

RAG operates in three stages. Think of it as giving the AI a research assistant that reads your documents before answering:

1

Indexing (one-time setup)

Your documents are broken into smaller chunks (paragraphs or sections). Each chunk is converted into a mathematical representation called an embedding — a list of numbers that captures the meaning of the text. These embeddings are stored in a special database called a vector store.

2

Retrieval (every question)

When you ask a question, your question is also converted into an embedding. The vector store finds the document chunks whose embeddings are most similar to your question's embedding. This is semantic search — it finds content that's related by meaning, not just keyword matching.

3

Generation (the answer)

The retrieved chunks are combined with your question and sent to the LLM. The AI now has the relevant context from your documents, so it can generate an answer that's both knowledgeable and grounded in your specific data.

A visual analogy

🧠

Without RAG

Like asking a brilliant expert a question — they're smart and well-read, but they haven't seen your specific documents. They'll give a good general answer, but may miss details that matter to you.

🧠📄

With RAG

Like giving that same expert your documents to read first, then asking the question. Now they can reference your specific data, cite exact passages, and give answers tailored to your situation.

Why RAG matters

🎯

Accuracy

Responses are grounded in real data rather than general training knowledge, dramatically reducing hallucinations.

🔒

Privacy

Your documents stay local — they're processed and stored on your machine, not sent to external training pipelines.

⚡

Always current

Upload new documents anytime. Unlike retraining a model (which takes weeks and costs millions), RAG updates instantly.

📎

Verifiable

RAG can show you exactly which documents and passages it used, so you can verify the answer yourself.

Real-world use cases

  • Company knowledge bases — Let employees ask questions about internal policies, product docs, and procedures
  • Legal document review — Search and query across contracts, case law, and regulatory filings
  • Research assistance — Upload papers and ask questions that connect findings across multiple studies
  • Customer support — Power chatbots with product documentation so they give accurate, specific answers
  • Personal productivity — Query your own notes, meeting transcripts, and saved articles
  • Financial analysis — Upload statements and reports, then ask questions about trends, totals, and anomalies

The technology behind this app

Vector Database ChromaDB

Stores document embeddings locally for fast semantic search

Embeddings all-MiniLM-L6-v2

Converts text into meaning-capturing vectors, runs locally on your machine

Language Model Google Gemini

Generates both the standard and RAG-informed responses

Backend Python / FastAPI

Handles document processing, chunking, and API orchestration
